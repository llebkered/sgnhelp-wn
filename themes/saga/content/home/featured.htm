Walk Throughs, Tips and Help for Jam City Games

Videos, Hints and Tips for Jam City Games: Cookie Jam, Cookie Jam Blast, Family Guy, Genies & Gems, Juice Jam, Snoopy Pop, Sugar Smash: Book of Life, Panda Pop and Paint Monsters
We are constantly adding more Episodes and Levels to the games.

Find the level you want by clicking the menu icon (top left). A menu will slide out and you can select the game.

Episodes for each game are listed on the games page.
Click on the episode containing your level number find videos of each Level.
Tips will be added to the more difficult Levels.

We are constantly adding new levels so if you can't find the one you're interested in, feel free to contact us at our Jam City Games Level Help FB page
